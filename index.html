Do I have to do anything?

Not really. We build the site from what's already known about your business. You just look it over and tell us if anything should change.

What if I don't like it?

Then you don't pay. You only pay once you've seen the finished site and want it.

Can I use my own photos?

Yes, and we recommend it. Previews sometimes use sample photos until you send yours. Reply with a few and we swap them in.

Who owns the web address?

You do. If you already have one, we connect it. If not, we help you get one in your name.

Can I make changes later?

Anytime. Email what you'd like changed. Small updates like new hours, prices or photos are included.
